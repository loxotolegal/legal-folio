Open issue · §9.1 termination for cause

What counts as cause

Their draft adds material underperformance to the list of triggers. That phrase is not defined anywhere in the agreement, so it would be read against whoever is holding the pen. We asked for it to be struck or defined.

9.1  The Company may terminate this Agreement for Cause upon written notice. Cause means fraud, a material breach of Section 10, conviction of a felony, or material underperformance.
